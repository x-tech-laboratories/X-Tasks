# History

X-Tasks keeps a **version of a task every time it changes**: its title, notes, dates, repeat, tags, relationships,
done state and place in the list. Nothing you change is lost.

## Browsing history

- The **History** view in the sidebar lists recent changes to every task, day by day. Tasks that have since been
  deleted are marked.
- The **History** button in a task's details lists every version of that one task.

Each version shows what changed from the one before it, down to the **individual words** of the title and notes.

## Restoring

Pick a version and choose **Restore this version**. Restoring never throws anything away: it saves the old version as
a new, latest version, so you can undo a restore the same way.

A **deleted task** can be brought back with **Restore task as this version**, from the History view.

## History on several devices

History travels with [sync](sync-and-backups.html#sync): a change made on your phone shows in the desktop app's
history too, and restoring on one device restores everywhere.

## Keeping history small

History is kept forever unless you turn on **Delete old history** in Settings › Data › History and choose **Days of
history to keep**. Older versions are then deleted on every device, while each task always keeps its newest version.
A deleted task can only be restored while its last version is kept.
